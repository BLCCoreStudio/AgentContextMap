# Code of Conduct

BLCCoreStudio projects are technical communities. Discussions, issues, reviews, and contributions should stay respectful, evidence-driven, and useful.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, including when you disagree.
- Critique code, designs, benchmarks, documentation, and evidence rather than people.
- Give others room to correct mistakes and ask questions.
- Keep reports relevant and avoid spam or low-effort bulk submissions.
- Follow each repository's documented safety, security, and responsible-disclosure boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory or demeaning language, threats, doxxing, deliberate misinformation, malicious disruption, or publishing private or security-sensitive information without permission are not acceptable.

## Security and sensitive research

Potential vulnerabilities should be reported according to `SECURITY.md` rather than disclosed publicly before maintainers have had a reasonable opportunity to investigate. Security, reverse-engineering, root, or device-tooling work must stay within the repository's documented scope and applicable authorization boundaries.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and productive.
